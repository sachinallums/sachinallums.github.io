About

As a kid, I was constantly designing games. Whether they were good (rarely) or bad (frequently), my dad would play test them with me and encourage me to keep thinking of new ideas. I absolutely love board and card games, particularly social party games, and whenever I have time on my hands, that’s what I think about. I attribute a lot of my creativity to this process of coming up with new ideas, and this, coupled with both formal and volunteer teaching experiences, led me to my current research interests.

I grew up in Austin, Texas, but I have become a California ride or die thanks to my time spent in the Bay Area for college. I am now living in Mokpo, South Korea, where I’ve gotten to see the most incredible cherry blossoms, hike a lot, and get better at Korean.

Fun Facts:

  • My first job was as a lifeguard when I was in high school
  • I studied German for 7 years from Middle School through my first year of college. Why did I pick German, you might ask? My friends wanted to take it, there was an exchange program, and my older sister told me to (and I was a pretty obedient younger brother).
  • When my sister left for college, she also left a sourdough starter at home, so I took on the responsibility to become the bread baker for the family. This has led to my love for baking, and my favorite things to bake are Italian ricciarelli cookies, chocolate miso sesame cookies, garlic naan, and Tres Leches cakes.
  • At one point, I lived with 10 roommates at a co-op in California, where I got really good at making meals for a large number of people. Unfortunately, I now struggle to cook for just 1 or 2 people.
  • I can juggle! This was a skill I picked up during the pandemic.
  • My more niche hobbies include memorizing the top ten of the Billboard 100 and 200 every week as well as playing Enclose Horse and finding Leif Penguinson.

Sports

I am a huge swimmer, and although I’m not the best, I’ve taught more than 15 of my college friends either how to swim or how to refine their stroke. I absolutely love the water, and I had a brief stint as a water polo player, but I quickly learned I am not built for that sport by any means.

Through middle school and briefly into high school, I played soccer, and my students in Korea have asked me to play soccer with them from time to time (to which I always say yes!). I grew up playing badminton casually with my dad, and my mom’s side of the family has instilled a love of playing ping pong too. Playing badminton and ping pong have contributed to a newfound love for pickleball, which I am getting better at thanks to playing with my boyfriend. I also love volleyball, ultimate frisbee, spikeball, and hacky sack. Although I am the namesake of Sachin Tendulkar, the best cricket player in history, I have yet to play cricket (although that’s next on my list!).
