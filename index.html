Hello!

I am a researcher, teacher, swimmer, baker, concert-goer, and current Fulbright scholar in South Korea interested in pursuing a PhD in the learning sciences. Before Fulbright, I graduated with a B.S. in Symbolic Systems from Stanford and worked as a predoctoral research coordinator with Dr. Mike Frank at the Language and Cognition Lab. I am applying to PhD programs during the 2026-2027 cycle, so if you think I would be a good fit for your lab, I would love to chat more!

Research Interests

My research interests span Education, Developmental Psychology, Cognitive Science, and Computer Science with a focus on creativity, social justice and technology. Specifically, I am interested in answering the following questions:

  • How can our current measurements of creativity accurately assess children in a world of AI?
  • How do children use technology for creative purposes? How are they harmed by this use?
  • What cognitive mechanisms explain how children approach creative tasks?
  • How can teachers encourage more creativity in the classroom without the use of technology?
  • In what ways do traditional assessments of creativity disadvantage low-SES students?

These questions are constantly evolving as I read, learn, and research more. I have a background in Developmental Psychology and quantitative methods, but I am excited to learn more Qualitative techniques for Learning Sciences research in the future.
